Veyline quickstart

Start free, get an API key, and make your first calls. The free Sandbox tier includes 1,000 operations per month and the full safety foundation.

  1. Start your free sandbox. In the Veyline pricing section, enter your email and click Start Free. Your API key is shown once — copy it immediately; it cannot be recovered later.
  2. Check your account. curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" https://payload-rail.fly.dev/v1/veyline/status returns your org, tier, and status. .../v1/veyline/usage shows operations used against your included allocation.
  3. Run a hosted action on your subscription. curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"intent":"purchase","target":"api.example.com/data","max_spend_usdc":"0.05"}' https://payload-rail.fly.dev/v1/callx402/actions/preflight authorizes a preflight check metered to your subscription and returns an invocation id; the action itself executes in your callx402 runtime. List all actions with GET https://payload-rail.fly.dev/v1/callx402/actions.

Every call sends your key as Authorization: Bearer <key>. Keys are product-bound to Veyline. The full machine-readable API reference is in agents.json.
