How Payload products are sold

Flagship platforms (Veyline, RevRule, callx402) are subscribed or licensed through their own product pages. Focused tools are one-time purchases with a single-seat perpetual license, sold on Gumroad and Whop; both storefronts carry the same product under the same terms. The full machine-readable catalog is products.json.

New products are added here only when they are finished, documented, and purchasable. Experiments stay off the catalog.
