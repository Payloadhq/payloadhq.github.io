The problem

Agents are moving money without a safety layer

The x402 protocol solved how software pays. Nobody solved what happens after it pays. Teams building agent commerce today face the same failure modes, over and over:

Duplicate payments

A retry after a network timeout becomes a second charge. A copied transaction hash becomes a second claim. Without atomic replay protection, the same money gets spent twice.

Uncertain settlement

Was the payment actually settled, or just submitted? Most integrations treat "sent" as "done." When settlement is ambiguous, operations proceed on hope — and hope isn't an accounting method.

No recovery path

Payment succeeded but the response never arrived. The operation is done, the money is gone, and the caller has no way to find out what happened. Retry, and you risk paying again.

No evidence trail

When something goes wrong — and it will — there's no tamper-evident record of what was authorized, what was paid, what was delivered, and what the outcome was. Disputes become arguments.

How it works

One operation, fully tracked

Every economic operation through Veyline follows the same lifecycle. Each stage is verified before the next begins, and the full trail is preserved as evidence.

INTENT→ POLICY→ PREFLIGHT→ ROUTING→ AUTHORIZATION→ PAYMENT→ SETTLEMENT→ EXECUTION→ DELIVERY→ OUTCOME→ RECONCILIATION→ RECOVERY→ EVIDENCE

Before the money moves

Intent is checked against your policies. Preflight simulation confirms the operation is viable. Nothing executes without authorization.

While the money moves

Payment is verified on-chain. Settlement is classified with certainty — confirmed, unconfirmed, or unknown. Execution runs exactly once, even under retries and crashes.

After the money moves

Delivery and outcome are proven separately from payment. Failures recover safely. The entire operation is recorded as tamper-evident evidence you can audit later.

Assurance model

Safety is the foundation, not a tier

Every Veyline customer gets the full safety model on every plan. Protection against duplicate payments, uncertain settlement, and silent failures is not something we sell as an upgrade.

Duplicate protection

  • Atomic transaction-hash reservation prevents double-claims
  • Idempotency keys make retries safe — same request, one effect
  • Concurrent submissions resolve to exactly one outcome

Settlement certainty

  • Payments verified independently on-chain, not trusted on submission
  • Settlement classified as confirmed, unconfirmed, or unknown
  • Unknown settlement never silently becomes "paid"

Evidence integrity

  • Every operation recorded in a hash-chained ledger
  • Payment, execution, delivery, and outcome proven separately
  • Deterministic replay reconstructs any operation for audit

Safe retry & recovery

  • Failed operations recover to a known state — never half-done
  • No retry after unknown settlement without explicit review
  • Crash-safe: interrupted operations resume or roll back cleanly

Policy enforcement

  • Intent checked against your policies before authorization
  • Budgets enforced before execution, approval thresholds that fail closed
  • Server-side enforcement — client assertions are never trusted

Credential security

  • High-entropy API keys, stored hashed, shown once
  • Scoped and product-bound — a key can't exceed its entitlement
  • Revocable and rotatable at any time

FAQ

Questions

Is Veyline a wallet?
No. Veyline never holds your funds, your private keys, or your seed phrases. Your wallet stays yours. Veyline verifies payments on-chain and controls what your agents are authorized to do — it never takes custody.
Do I get Veyline's source code?
No. You purchase licensed access to Veyline's capabilities — the API, the CLI, the documentation, and your entitled features. The underlying platform, including its safety systems and proprietary logic, remains operated by Payload.
What chains and assets are supported?
At launch: USDC on Base. Additional networks and assets will be added as demand and verification support them.
How is Veyline different from the Veyline Developer Primer?
The Developer Primer ($79) is the downloadable starter kit for learning x402 and adding payments to your own server — you run everything yourself. Veyline is the hosted production platform that evolved from that direction: the full economic lifecycle with settlement certainty, exactly-once execution, recovery, and evidence, operated as a service.
Do my agents need me to approve every payment?
No. Once you've configured your policies, spend limits, and entitlements, your agents operate autonomously inside them. Purchasing access is a one-time human step; economic operations run on their own within your approved bounds.
What happens if a payment succeeds but something fails afterward?
Veyline recovers the operation to a known state. You can query the operation to see exactly what happened — what was paid, what executed, what was delivered — and retry safely without risk of duplicate charges.
Is there a security or architecture brief for procurement?
Yes. The enterprise brief covers use cases, production architecture, security and governance evidence, deployment, support expectations, and a procurement checklist. It states plainly what is and is not claimed, and prints cleanly for review.
Is RevRule included with Veyline?
No. RevRule is a separate Payload product with its own purchase and entitlement. Veyline works fully without it; the two can integrate where that makes sense for your use case.
What if Stripe isn't available in my country?
Use the USDC path: it works for any plan, in any country. Open the contact form from the purchase section above, tell us which plan you want, and we'll arrange a crypto checkout in USDC on Base. Your API key is issued the same way — shown once, ready to use immediately.
