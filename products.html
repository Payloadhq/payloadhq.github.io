Payload Flow

Programmable revenue infrastructure. Define a Revenue Graph (participants, rules, versions). Send economic events. Get auditable entitlements on a hash-chained ledger.

  • Who gets paid, computed deterministically: percentages, fixed amounts, referrals, recoupment waterfalls, caps, attribution.
  • State persists across transactions. Attribution references earlier events. Rules version with an approval gate.
  • x402 machine payments accepted: USDC on Base, verified on-chain.
  • Open-source engine (MIT). Hosted API for zero-ops use.

Flow computes who is owed what. Your Stripe account or payment rail executes the payouts. Flow never holds funds.
