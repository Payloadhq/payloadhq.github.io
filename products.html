RevRule by Payload

PROGRAMMABLE ECONOMIC INFRASTRUCTURE

RevRule is a programmable revenue rules engine for deciding who earns what whenever your product, API, platform, marketplace, or project makes money.

Create persistent rules for royalties, revenue shares, referrals, commissions, recoupment, waterfalls, contributor payouts, platform fees, and API/x402 revenue. RevRule remembers economic state across transactions, applies the correct rules when new revenue events occur, and produces an auditable record of what each participant is entitled to.

Your payment rail moves the money. RevRule determines the economics.

Use RevRule for:

  • software contributor royalties
  • API and AI-agent revenue sharing
  • marketplace seller/platform economics
  • affiliate and referral payouts
  • recoupment and revenue waterfalls
  • creator/media royalties and residuals
  • agency commissions
  • licensing and digital-product revenue sharing
  • Who earns what, computed deterministically: percentages, fixed amounts, referrals, recoupment waterfalls, caps, attribution.
  • State persists across transactions. Attribution references earlier events. Rules version with an approval gate.
  • x402 machine payments accepted: USDC on Base, verified on-chain.
  • Open-source engine (MIT). Hosted API for zero-ops use.

Non-custodial by architecture: RevRule computes entitlements; it never holds funds.
