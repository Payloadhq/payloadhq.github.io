Software category

AI Search & Discoverability

Tools that check whether websites can be found, read, and cited by AI search engines and answer systems.

About this category

The problems it addresses

Search is no longer only a list of links. AI answer engines crawl pages, decide whether they can be cited, and quote the sources they trust. Websites that crawlers cannot reach, or whose structured data is dead, quietly disappear from those answers.

This category audits a page the way an AI search system experiences it: crawler coverage, citation eligibility, robots.txt access, and schema health, checked in the browser with nothing uploaded anywhere.

Common problems

Sound familiar?

  • Pages blocked or misread by AI crawlers without the owner knowing
  • Structured data that exists but is invalid, dead, or never rendered
  • No quick way to check a page before publishing it

Choosing well

Buying guidance

Audit your highest-value pages first: the ones you would want quoted in an answer. Fix crawler access before content changes; a page an engine cannot fetch cannot be cited, however good it is.
