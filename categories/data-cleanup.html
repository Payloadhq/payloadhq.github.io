Data Cleanup & Migration

Offline tools that deduplicate and normalize business data before it moves to a new system.

About this category

The problems it addresses

Every CRM migration inherits the duplicates already in the export: the same person under three spellings, the same company under two names. Importing them as-is makes the duplicates permanent in the new system, where they are far harder to remove.

Software here works offline on the export itself: fuzzy matching with confidence tiers, clustering of every variant of a record, and a dry-run merge plan you review before anything changes. Your customer list never leaves your machine.

Common problems

Sound familiar?

  • Duplicate people and companies under variant spellings in CRM exports
  • Cloud dedup tools that require uploading an entire customer list to a third party
  • Migrations that go ahead without a reviewable merge plan

Choosing well

Buying guidance

Always review the merge plan before applying it, and keep the exported change log: it is your audit trail for every merge decision the tool made. Clean the export before the import, not after.
