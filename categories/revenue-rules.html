Revenue Rules & Royalties

Software that decides who earns what when money moves: royalties, revenue shares, commissions, recoupment, and payout verification.

About this category

The problems it addresses

When more than one party earns from the same revenue, someone has to decide who earned what, in what order, under which rules. This category is that decision layer: revenue rules, royalty splits, recoupment, and payout verification, recorded in an auditable ledger.

RevRule is the product in this category. It calculates entitlements from rules you define and records every result. It never holds, transfers, or distributes funds: your payment system still moves the money, and RevRule establishes what it should move.

Common problems

Sound familiar?

  • Royalties, revenue shares, and commissions calculated by hand or in spreadsheets
  • Recoupment and waterfall rules that change state as revenue arrives
  • Payouts that cannot be verified against the entitlements they were supposed to satisfy

Choosing well

Buying guidance

If your splits are simple percentages that never change, a spreadsheet may honestly be enough. When rules carry state, such as recoupment thresholds, caps, or qualifying conditions, or when a partner disputes a payout, a rules engine with a verifiable ledger earns its place.
