Automation & Agent Reliability

Production-readiness systems for workflow automations and AI agents: guardrails, failure simulation, and readiness scoring.

About this category

The problems it addresses

Workflow automations and AI agents fail quietly: a node times out, a tool call loops, a spend limit that existed on paper is never enforced. This category is the production-readiness layer for n8n workflows and the agents inside them.

The kit here combines guardrail templates, a failure-simulation evaluation harness, and a readiness score, so a workflow's weaknesses surface in testing instead of in production, at 3 a.m., with a customer watching.

Common problems

Sound familiar?

  • n8n AI agents with no spend caps, tool limits, or failure alerts
  • No way to simulate failures before a workflow goes live
  • Readiness judged by optimism instead of by a repeatable score

Choosing well

Buying guidance

Start with the two free sample workflows on GitHub to see the pattern, then apply the guardrail templates to your highest-risk automation: the one that spends money or talks to customers.
