Questions

What RevRule is not

Does RevRule hold my money?
No. RevRule never holds funds and never executes payouts. It computes who is entitled to what. Your payment rail moves the money. RevRule determines the economics.
Do I need crypto?
No. RevRule works with ordinary payment rails. Crypto and x402 are supported where they fit, never required.
Can I use it with normal software?
Yes. If your product can call an API when revenue happens, RevRule can program the economics.
Is it only for royalties?
No. Royalties are one use case. Revenue shares, referrals, commissions, recoupment, waterfalls, platform fees, and contributor payouts all run on the same engine.
Is it only for x402?
No. x402 is one way economic events enter RevRule. Any revenue event works.
Is it only for large companies?
No. A solo API builder with one referrer gets the same engine as a marketplace.
Do I have to replace my payment provider?
No. Keep Stripe, keep your rail. RevRule sits beside it and programs the economics on top.
