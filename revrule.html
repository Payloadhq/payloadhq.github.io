RevRule is a revenue rules engine for businesses where more than one party earns from the same revenue. Define your contract rules once: who gets what share, what gets recouped first, how a waterfall flows. Every time revenue comes in, RevRule calculates each participant's entitlement and records it in an auditable ledger.

RevRule calculates entitlements and records the results. It never holds your funds and never executes payments. Your payment system still moves the money.

How it works

Define the rules once. RevRule runs them on every payment.

Three steps replace the spreadsheets, the hand-tracked recoupment, and the payout logic scattered across your codebase.

Step 1

Define a Revenue Graph

Encode the economics of a deal as a persistent rule set: percentages, recoupment, waterfalls, caps, and attribution. The rules belong to the relationship, not to one payment.

Step 2

Send revenue events

When revenue happens, your product sends one event. Stripe, x402, or any other rail: the rail can change, the economics persist.

Step 3

RevRule calculates and records

Each participant's entitlement is calculated against the current state of the rules and written to a hash-chained ledger you can query and export as JSON.

FAQ

Straight answers

What RevRule does, what it does not do, and exactly how buying it works.

What does RevRule do?
RevRule calculates who is entitled to what when revenue is shared. You define the rules of a deal once, as a Revenue Graph: percentages, recoupment, waterfalls, caps, attribution. When a revenue event arrives, RevRule applies the current state of those rules and records each participant's entitlement in a hash-chained ledger that you can query and export as JSON.
Does RevRule hold my money?
No. RevRule never holds funds and never executes payouts. It computes who is entitled to what. Your payment system moves the money. RevRule decides who earns what.
What is the difference between the $99 license and a managed plan?
The $99 license is perpetual and uncapped: unlimited Revenue Graphs, no monthly operations cap, one API key, never metered, never expires. A managed plan is a monthly hosted service with a set capacity tier: a monthly operations allowance, a set number of Revenue Graphs, and up to 10 API keys on the Business tier. The two combine cleanly. A plan never reduces a license, and cancelling a plan never takes one away.
How do I cancel a managed plan?
Cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and your plan allowances stay active until that date. Partial-period payments are not refunded. If you also hold the $99 one-time license, it is completely unaffected.
What is the refund policy?
Eligible new purchases on Payload controlled surfaces carry a 7-day refund window. Charges made in error are refunded in full. On marketplaces such as Whop or Gumroad, the marketplace's own refund rules govern the purchase.
What support is included?
Email support on every paid option, with best-effort response. We do not promise guaranteed response times. Write to us any time at the support address in the footer.
Do I need crypto?
No. RevRule works with ordinary payment rails. Crypto and x402 are supported where they fit, never required.
Can I use it with normal software?
Yes. If your product can call an API when revenue happens, RevRule can program the economics.
Is it only for royalties?
No. Royalties are one use case. Revenue shares, referrals, commissions, recoupment, waterfalls, platform fees, and contributor payouts all run on the same engine.
Is it only for x402?
No. x402 is one way economic events enter RevRule. Any revenue event works.
Is it only for large companies?
No. A solo API builder with one referrer gets the same engine as a marketplace.
Do I have to replace my payment provider?
No. Keep Stripe, keep your rail. RevRule sits beside it and programs the economics on top.
