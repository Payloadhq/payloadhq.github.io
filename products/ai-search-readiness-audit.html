The problem

Why this exists

Pages can rank in classic search yet be invisible to AI answer engines because the raw HTML lacks the content, AI crawlers are blocked in robots.txt, or the structured data on the page is dead. None of that is visible from the rendered page.

Who it is for

Built for

Site owners, marketers, and developers checking whether AI search systems can find and cite their pages.
