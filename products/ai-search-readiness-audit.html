AI Search Readiness Audit

Audit any page for AI-search readiness, right in Chrome: coverage, eligibility, crawler access, and dead schema.

The problem it solves

Pages can rank in classic search yet be invisible to AI answer engines because the raw HTML lacks the content, AI crawlers are blocked in robots.txt, or the structured data on the page is dead. None of that is visible from the rendered page.

Who it is for

Site owners, marketers, and developers checking whether AI search systems can find and cite their pages.

What it does

  • Raw-HTML coverage check: what AI crawlers actually receive versus what the browser renders
  • AI Overviews eligibility signals on the page you are viewing
  • AI-crawler robots.txt matrix across the major search and answer bots
  • Dead schema detection for structured data that no longer resolves
  • 100% client-side: the audit runs in your browser on the page you are viewing

Platforms and inputs

Platforms: Chrome (Manifest V3)

Works with: Any web page viewed in Chrome

Version: 1.0.1
