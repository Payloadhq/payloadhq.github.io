CRM Dedup System

Fully offline deduplication and normalization for CRM exports, before a migration makes duplicates permanent.

The problem it solves

CRM exports carry duplicate people and companies under variant spellings. Migrating them as-is poisons the new system, and cloud dedup tools require uploading your entire customer list to a third party.

Who it is for

Teams migrating between CRMs, and operators cleaning a CRM export before import.

What it does

  • Fuzzy matching with confidence tiers, so obvious duplicates and judgment calls are separated
  • Union-find clustering that groups every variant of the same record together
  • Dry-run merge plans you review before anything changes
  • Import-ready CSV export plus machine-readable change logs of every merge decision
  • Runs fully offline as a single-file binary with a local web UI and CLI

Platforms and inputs

Platforms: Linux (single-file binary), Local web UI, CLI

Works with: CRM CSV exports

Questions

Does my customer data leave my machine?

No. The system runs fully offline; exports are processed locally and nothing is uploaded.

Can I review merges before applying them?

Yes. It produces a dry-run merge plan first, and the final export comes with a machine-readable change log.
