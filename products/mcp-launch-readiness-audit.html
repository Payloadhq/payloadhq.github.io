MCP Launch Readiness Audit

A 48-rule security and readiness scanner for MCP servers, with a fix for every finding.

The problem it solves

MCP servers ship with tool poisoning risks, hardcoded credentials, exposed endpoints, over-privileged tools, SSRF paths, and missing authentication, and most teams have no checklist that catches them before launch.

Who it is for

Teams about to publish or sell an MCP server.

What it does

  • Zero-dependency CLI scanner with 48 detection rules: tool poisoning, hardcoded credentials, server exposure, over-privileged tools, SSRF, missing auth and hardening
  • A concrete remediation for every finding, not just a warning
  • Hardened templates, a stress-test harness, a CI workflow, and a branded audit report
  • An audit, not a certification: it reports what it finds so you can fix and re-run
