Payload affiliate program terms

Last updated: October 16, 2026. These are the published terms of the Payload affiliate program. The program runs through Whop, and affiliates enroll through the affiliate section of Payload's Whop storefront.

Commission rates

Commissions are a percentage of the sale price of each referred sale, paid in USD. Rates are set per product; any Payload product sold on Whop that is not listed below earns the store default rate of 30%.

Payouts and currency

All commissions are tracked and paid in USD through Whop. Payout timing, payout methods, and payout thresholds are set by Whop under Whop's affiliate terms, not by Payload. See the affiliate section of our Whop storefront: whop.com/payload-f126/affiliates.

Commissions are earned on completed referred sales. There is no payment for clicks or signups.

How to enroll

Enroll through the affiliate section of Payload's Whop storefront: whop.com/payload-f126/affiliates. Once enrolled, Whop issues your affiliate links and tracks your referrals.

Program rules

As a Payload program requirement, promoters must disclose their affiliate relationship when promoting Payload products. Promoters must also describe products accurately, based on the public product pages. Payload may end the program or change rates with notice; commissions already earned are paid out.

Contact

Questions about the program: contact@payloadhq.dev.
