Customer-controlled Safe pilot

Veyline's supported owner-mediated payment path validates exact payment intent, verifies a signed authorization, atomically claims it before the wallet signs, and reconciles the matching blockchain receipt. This requires an enrolled organization and approved executor integration, not a generic browser send button.

Safe owners can independently transact through other applications. Veyline does not impose an on-chain Guard or claim universal wallet-level blocking. The first supported pilot uses a threshold of one signer.
