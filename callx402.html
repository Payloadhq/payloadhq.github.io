How it works

Start free on your own machine. Go hosted when you want us to run it.

Three steps between a failed payment and an answer you can act on. This is the original callx402, the diagnostic path; the workspace above runs these same actions for you in the browser.

Step 1

Run the free status check

Install the npm package and run npx callx402. The free x402_status tool reads your setup and reports what it finds, locally, in seconds. No account, no payment.

Step 2

Diagnose locally

The package ships six local MCP tools (status, diagnose, evidence, explain, recover, resolve) that work against your own x402 payment failure. They are read only and never move money.

Step 3

Or call a hosted action

Ask the free quote endpoint for the exact price, then call a hosted diagnostic action on the Payload rail and pay per action in USDC on Base. You get structured evidence and an explanation back.

FAQ

Straight answers

What callx402 does, what it does not do, and exactly how paying for it works.

What is callx402, in plain terms?
callx402 gives you AI agents that can do real tasks, in a workspace that runs in your browser. You can run ready-made agent capabilities, connect a wallet so agents can pay for what they use under limits you set, and look for funded jobs your agents can take on. It began as a toolkit for diagnosing failed x402 payments, and that toolkit is still here: free on your own machine, pay per action when Payload runs it.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. The workspace is the no-code way in: create an account, open the application, and run capabilities from the browser. The coding route still exists for developers (the npm package, the APIs, and the MCP server), but nothing about the workspace requires it.
What does the $9 a month subscription cover?
The Agent Workspace: ready-made agent capabilities and your agents in one browser workspace, wallet connection with your own spending controls, 100 included Class A hosted actions per month, and 1 API key. It does not cover per-action fees beyond that allowance, and it is never required to look for jobs or to receive job compensation.
What costs extra beyond the subscription?
Hosted actions are pay per action. Each one shows its exact price before you commit: fractions of a dollar in USDC on Base, with the card schedule at twenty times the USDC price. The free quote endpoint prices any action first, the local tools cost nothing, and there is no balance to top up and no charge for browsing jobs.
How does the wallet work?
You connect your own wallet. callx402 is non-custodial, so Payload never holds your keys or your funds, and you are never asked for a seed phrase. You set spending limits and approval thresholds, and every payment an agent makes runs under those controls. Payments for hosted actions and compensation from jobs settle in USDC on Base.
How do jobs and earnings work?
Find Work looks for jobs that a person or business has independently funded. You review a job before accepting it, the agent does the work in your workspace, and the result is delivered for verification. Compensation comes from the job's funder and is paid to your wallet; a job is only marked paid after that payment actually settles. Jobs are not guaranteed: the verified inventory can be empty, and when it is, the workspace says so plainly instead of showing filler.
What is x402? Do I need to understand it?
x402 is the payment protocol the agents use to pay per action over HTTP. If you use the workspace, you do not need to understand it: prices are shown before anything runs. Developers who want the protocol details will find them in the documentation section below.
What does callx402 do?
It diagnoses failed x402 payments. Give it a failure, locally through the npm package or through a hosted action on the Payload rail, and it classifies which layer broke, returns structured evidence, explains the failure in plain language, and identifies the safest next step. It is also the workspace described above: those same capabilities run there as ready-made agents for people who would rather not wire up the tools themselves.
Is callx402 free?
The npm package is free, including the x402_status check and the six local tools. Hosted actions are pay per use: fractions of a dollar in USDC on Base, priced at one twentieth of the card schedule shown above. An optional Agent Workspace subscription at $9 per month covers the workspace, 100 included Class A hosted actions per month, and 1 API key; it is never required for the free local tools or for looking for jobs.
Does callx402 hold or move my money?
No. The local tools are read only and never move money. A hosted action is a diagnostic you pay for; callx402 returns evidence and explanations, not custody, settlement, or execution of your payments.
Can callx402 recover a failed payment?
No tool can promise that, and callx402 does not. It tells you what the evidence shows and what the safest next step is. Some settled failures cannot be reversed, and an honest diagnosis says so.
How do I pay for a hosted action?
Call the action and it returns an x402 challenge with the exact USDC amount and the payTo on Base. Pay from your own wallet and redeem, and the rail verifies the payment once, on chain, before returning the result. Card payment is available through the rail checkout endpoint. The free quote endpoint always gives the exact price first.
Can I use callx402 as an MCP server?
Yes. callx402 is published as an MCP server (io.github.payload-tools/callx402) and listed on Smithery and the official MCP Registry. Install the npm package to run the tools locally.
What support is included?
Email support on a best effort basis. We do not promise guaranteed response times. Write to us any time at the support address in the footer.
