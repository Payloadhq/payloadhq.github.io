Refund Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Digital software purchases and subscription payments are generally non-refundable. You may cancel a subscription at any time and retain access through the end of your current paid billing period. Cancellation prevents future renewal charges. This policy does not affect any refund, cancellation, or other rights provided by applicable law.

1. Where this policy applies

This policy applies to purchases made directly from Payload on payloadhq.dev and through Payload's own Stripe checkout. It does not apply to purchases made on marketplaces such as Gumroad, Whop, or AppSumo. Those purchases are governed by the marketplace's own terms and refund rules. This policy applies to purchases made on or after November 12, 2026. Earlier purchases keep the refund terms shown when they were made.

2. Downloadable software and perpetual licenses

All downloadable software and perpetual license purchases are final sale once access or delivery has been provided. We do not offer refunds because you changed your mind, because a one-time task is complete, or because you no longer need the software. The exceptions in section 5 always apply.

3. Subscriptions

Subscription payments are non-refundable as a general policy, subject to the exceptions in section 5. You may cancel a subscription at any time from your account or through the billing portal linked from your account. When you cancel, automatic renewal stops and no further recurring charge is made after the current paid period. You keep your subscription access until the exact end of the current billing period, and access then expires unless another valid entitlement applies. No prorated refund is issued unless the law requires one. Example: if your plan renews on December 12 and you cancel on November 20, your access continues through December 12 and then ends.

4. Charges made in error

Duplicate charges and charges made in error are refunded in full.

5. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right that cannot be excluded under applicable law. In particular, this policy does not affect: refunds or other remedies required by law for software that is defective, not as described, or never delivered; consumer guarantees that apply in Australia and New Zealand; the seven day right of regret for online purchases in Brazil, which we honor for downloads and first subscription purchases regardless of delivery; and withdrawal rights in the European Union and the United Kingdom, as described in section 6.

6. Withdrawal rights for digital content in the EU and UK

At checkout, before delivery, you are asked to agree to immediate delivery and to acknowledge that, where the law gives you a right to withdraw from a digital content purchase, that right ends once delivery begins. Your statutory rights for digital content that is defective or not as described are not affected. Subscription access also starts immediately with your agreement; if you withdraw within a statutory withdrawal period, a proportionate amount may be payable for the period already supplied, as the law provides.

7. How to request a refund the law requires

Contact us through the support page with the email address used for the purchase and a short description of the issue. We review every request and reply. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Updates apply only to purchases made after the updated policy takes effect.
